V3.47 remains an experimental calculator, not a rules-complete Pathfinder 1e or AI GM implementation. Swift is tracked; arbitrary trigger interpretation and complex nested interrupts remain GM adjudications. Grab and Constrict are supported. Legacy fixtures use simplified statistics. Imported abilities are limited to the supported declarative vocabulary. The 50 × 40 ft demonstration board is too small to represent Vienna's full 360-foot aura. For that fixture, the exact 360-foot radius and 1d20 × 10-foot travel distance are applied in grid units, with relocation to the nearest legal map square.
